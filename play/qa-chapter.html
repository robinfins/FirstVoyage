Find your way.

At the Sunny stairs, hold W to climb or S to descend. Follow the gold markers to travel. Rest at snails to heal and save. Melee pirates pursue across platforms and can chain attacks. Bombs explode on impact. Press F to eat meat: one health segment per use, three portions replenished at snails. Rest to pause and travel between activated checkpoints. Five landed punches fill one bar. Specials require firm footing and lock your aim when started. Dash to cancel; spent bars are not refunded. Defeat Buggy to unlock Gatling: 18 punches in 1.8 seconds. Return to his arena marker and press E for a rematch.
