    Find your way.

    At the Sunny stairs, hold W to climb or S to descend. Follow the gold markers to travel. Rest at snails to heal and save. Melee pirates pursue across platforms and can chain attacks. Bombs explode on impact. After defeating Kuro, press X for Gear 2: 1/2/3 full bars give 3/7/13 seconds. Hold left click for rapid punches; right click uses Jet Stamp and ends the buff. Press F to eat meat: one health segment per use, three portions replenished at snails. Rest to pause and travel between activated checkpoints. Five landed punches fill one bar. Specials require firm footing and lock your aim when started. Dash to cancel; spent bars are not refunded. Defeat Buggy to unlock Gatling: 18 punches in 1.8 seconds. Return to his arena marker and press E for a rematch. Use Fullscreen to fill the screen so the aiming cursor stays over the fight; notifications move onto the canvas there, and Esc leaves fullscreen and pauses. Lock cursor captures the mouse outright so it cannot leave the game at all; click the canvas to re-arm it after Esc releases it. Each captain you defeat levels Luffy up: every attack hits harder and you gain a health segment. The score is synthesised as you play: a quiet waltz aboard the Sunny, a march on the routes, and a battle cue that gains a counter-line when a boss reaches phase two. Toggle it with Music. Sound turns on the effects: stretching punches, a rush of air on the dash, a cannon for Bazooka and eighteen dry cracks for Gatling.
