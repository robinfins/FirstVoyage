Find your way.

At the Sunny stairs, hold W to climb or S to descend. Follow the gold markers to travel. Rest at snails to heal and save. Melee pirates pursue across platforms and can chain attacks. Bombs explode on impact. Five landed punches fill one bar. Specials require firm footing and lock your aim when started. Dash to cancel; spent bars are not refunded. Gatling lasts three seconds.
